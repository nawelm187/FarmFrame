# Changelog

Newest first. Older entries keep their original wording.

- **v0.78** — The search now understands Spanish and the natural way of asking. A new word list (`lexicon.ts`, 26 materials and currencies with their Spanish and short names) turns "creditos", "plat", "celulas orokin" or "trazas del vacio" into the game's names before searching, and "mejor forma de farmear X" no longer reads "forma" as the item Forma. "How do I get X", "como encuentro este material X", "best way to get X fast" and "credit farm xoris" now open the right card; a new "what is X" / "que es X" / "para que sirve X" card shows what the thing is (type, tradable, description) and the ways to farm it. A material the person names wins over a fuzzy match, a subject nothing recognises is no longer shown as an item when a method already answers it, and methods whose main product is the material come first. 12 phrases that failed in v0.77 were measured and now pass. New `search.golden.test.ts` lists about 58 real phrases in Spanish and English with the answer each must give: add a line there whenever a search fails. Hand-checked only on the machine used for the build; nothing seen in a browser. Run no new SQL.
- **v0.77** — Farming Methods reaches 25 methods with five more materials read from the Warframe Wiki: Void Traces (Reactant in Void Fissures, 6-30 per run, with the wiki's own multiplier formula and the Granum Void), Vosfor (dissolving Arcanes with Loid for 12-98 each, The Icebind at 16.88% for 200, Kaya, syndicate packs), Gallium (Desdemona caches at 15.49% for 3), Nitain Extract (Nightwave sells 5 for 15 Cred, better than any drop) and Tellurium (Desdemona caches, Steel Path Ophelia). Two contradictions inside the wiki are written into the methods instead of hidden: the Plastids guide says Tellurium only drops in Archwing and Empyrean while the Tellurium page lists Star Chart tables, and the Void Traces page applies boosters to a reward its own infobox says they do not affect. The Aya method also lists the Necralisk rows at 22.22% and 28.57% found in the rest of the table. Run no new SQL.
- **v0.76** — Farming Methods reaches 20 methods and the source registry now records where strategies come from and how far to trust them. New methods from the Warframe Wiki guides: Orokin Cells (break every crate with Xaku or Limbo at Tethys; Nekros and Khora survival at Gabii; Exterminate fissures, the wiki's own estimate of at least one cell per run), Plastids (Ophelia, Uranus) and Argon Crystals (break every container in a Void Capture at Hepit or Ukko); Endo at Vodyanoi, where the wiki table says 250 Endo at 22% and two uncited articles claim 2,000-4,000 per run or 10,000+ per hour, both shown only as unmeasured claims; and a community entry with the solo Index frames players suggest on Steam (Rhino for its armor, Equinox, Nidus, Nova, Mesa). Five sources registered: wiki guides (C, active), Steam discussions (E), Warframe Forums (E, planned: four topics would not open), r/Warframe (E, planned: blocked) and guide articles (D, planned: they cite no measurements). Every rate stays per roll or per run unless a source measured time, and none did. Run no new SQL.
- **v0.75** — Farming Methods grows to 13 methods and now separates what was read on the wiki from what players report. New: Forma (best listed drop, Orokin Derelict Defense rotation C, 22.56%; Acrithis, Market and others), Orokin Cells (bosses and Sentients, plus buying or crafting), Steel Essence (Incursions, The Circuit, The Descendia, Acolytes), Endo from Ayatan Stars sold to Maroo, and **The Index with the Xoris heavy attack**, marked *Community strategy, not verified*: the Xoris stats and the Index rules come from the wiki, but the forum thread with the exact solo loadout could not be opened from the server and the Steam guide about it was removed, so FarmFrame does not claim the loadout, the speed or that allied bots' deposits count. Community methods always come after wiki methods in the answers and carry a warning tag. The Index method now says the wiki disagrees with itself on whether the Credit Booster doubles every round or only the first. The search finds methods by words such as "xoris index" and the page filters by material. Run no new SQL.
- **v0.74** — New section **Farming Methods** (Farm menu): eight hand-written methods for Credits, Aya, Endo and Kuva, each with what to do, what it needs first, which boosts apply and which do not, a companion mod card where one matters (Prosperous Retriever: 18% chance to double Credit pickups, Naramon, drain 2 to 7, bought from The Business in Fortuna) and the wiki page it comes from with the date it was read. Numbers are per roll or per run, never per hour, and the page says so. The search now answers "how do I get X" with what it is (type, tradable, description), the best verified sources, who sells it and the methods that give it; "fastest way to farm credits" or "mejor forma de farmear aya" opens a card with the method, its first steps and its boosts, and says plainly when no method is written for that material. The wiki pages were read through a summarizer and two tables (Aya, Endo) were truncated, so those methods are marked and may miss a better row. New files `data/methods.ts`, `lib/methods.ts`, `pages/Methods.tsx`; 3 new tests and new intent checks. Run no new SQL.
- **v0.73** — Shared identity for things: new `refs.ts` gives every Warframe, weapon, part, resource and relic one stable id (`warframe:rhino-prime`, `thing:forma-blueprint`, `relic:lith-a1`) and maps each of their in-app addresses (`/item/…`, `/farm/…`, `/resources?q=…`, `/relics?q=…`) back to it. Warframe and weapon ids equal the goal id, so search, goals and Roadmap agree. Search results now carry that id, search points parts and relic rewards to the one part page, and Tracking compares names ignoring case, accents and apostrophes ("Vauban's Chassis" = "vaubans chassis"). Nothing changes on screen yet; this is the base for linking Farm Plan, Roadmap and Tracking. 4 new tests. Run no new SQL.
- **v0.72** — Farm Now now says exactly which relic to open where: for each tier with your missing parts, the cards list "Open Lith G1 in Capture at Earth" for the relics you own, paired with the best fissure you can start (a normal mission before a Void Storm or an unchecked Steel Path node), and a collapsed list of every active fissure of that tier with its Ready / Check first tag. - **v0.71** — Farm Now now separates what you can do right now from what needs something first. Every fissure, invasion and activity gets a tag: Ready, Check first or Needs something. Reasons are listed under "Why this?" in Farm Now, the Planner, the Advisor's "Best activity right now" and the search card: a Void Storm or Proxima node asks you to check Railjack, a Steel Path fissure asks you to check Steel Path access, a node you have not ticked in the Mastery checklist asks you to check it, and an exact-relic match is marked "Needs" when you own none of the listed relics. Ready items always come first, then Check, then Needs, and inside each the old ranking is kept. These are checks derived from your Mastery ticks and the relics you marked as owned, not from the game itself; an unknown node or an empty checklist adds no warning. New file `unlock.ts`; 10 new tests. Run no new SQL.

- **v0.69** — The search is now a real smart search instead of a list of fixed sentences. One index covers pages (with their keywords), Warframes, weapons, companions, Archwings, mods, parts, relics and their rewards, resources, items, enemies, syndicate and Palladino offers, Arbitration rewards, Helminth abilities, guides and Nightwave rewards. It reads the query word by word: words in any order, cut short ("ash pri neur"), with typos ("wsip") or taken from a description or drop place ("invisible stealth frame", "sedna hydron") all find the right thing; filler words in English and Spanish are ignored; at least 60% of the words must match. Questions are read from their words, not from fixed phrases: "what should I farm now", "que farmear ahora" or "best thing to farm today" open a card with the best activity running now for your goals, where it is, which relics to open, why it is first and what else is running (it asks you to pick a goal when you have none, and says so when nothing running helps); "where do I get X", "donde conseguir X" or just typing an item name opens a card with the best verified places to get it, why the first one is first, and which vendors sell it; "what changed" shows the newest update. The older shortcuts (need, build, prerequisite, relics that contain) still appear as extra results. New files `smartSearch.ts`, `searchDocs.ts`, `SearchAnswers.tsx`; 13 new tests. Measured on made-up data of 7,500 entries: about 160 ms to build the index and 6 to 14 ms per search. Run no new SQL.
