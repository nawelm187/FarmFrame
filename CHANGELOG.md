- **v0.106** — Damage test under the build editor. **New:** for primary and secondary builds, a panel that tests the build against an enemy of the level you choose (pick one from the enemy data or type its base health, shields and armor; choose its scaling group, base level, level and Eximus). It shows the average damage per shot, DPS without and with reloads, shots to kill, reloads and time to kill, a per-type table (per pellet, vs health, vs shields) and the totals your mods give (critical, status, fire rate, multishot). Every base stat of the weapon can be edited; if the item data has no usable damage or fire rate the panel opens in manual mode. Melee shows a notice (it needs combos and stances). The rules are the WARFRAME Wiki's (modified base damage first, elements calculated on it and combined two by two in slot order, average critical multiplier, armor formula with the armor class modifier) and live in `src/lib/damage.ts` with 9 tests of the wiki's worked examples; the weapon's damage types are read with the same index table the item page uses (this caught my own wrong guess about the order of cold, electricity and heat). 32 new keys in all 9 languages. Details and limits in `docs/DAMAGE_TEST.md`. **Honest limits:** it is an estimate, **not compared with in-game values**; status effects, headshots, faction and conditional mods, armor stripping, shield gating and Overguard are not included, and the panel says so; the enemy modifiers come from the community data and may be older than the current game. **Not yet verified:** `npm test` and `npm run build` on your PC, and how the real weapon and enemy data fills the panel (I could not load them here).
- **v0.105** — Aura, Stance and Exilus slots in the build editor. **New:** a Warframe build has an Aura slot and an Exilus slot, a primary or secondary has an Exilus slot, and a melee weapon has a Stance slot and an Exilus slot. Each shows as a compact card with a dashed frame in its own color (Aura teal, Stance gold, Exilus purple), rank - / +, a polarity picker and a remove button; tap it to choose a mod from the picker, which now lists only the mods that fit that slot. Aura and Stance mods add capacity (doubled on a matching polarity, 25% less on a mismatch, per the current wiki); Exilus mods spend capacity and need the utility flag in the data (warned when the data does not say). Aura and Stance mods are no longer accepted in normal slots, which also fixes that a melee build could not place a Stance mod at all. Special-slot mods count in the duplicate check, the missing-mods list, the endo estimate, the stat totals and the data sent to the AI helper. New: `build.special.test.ts` (6 tests) and `docs/BUILD_SLOTS.md`. **Also:** a type check with stand-in definitions for React and the router, which I set up to catch mistakes in `.tsx` files from here, finds no errors in the project beyond the stand-ins' own noise. **Not done:** Forma for special-slot polarities is not counted; the damage-test panel is next. **Not yet verified:** `npm test` and `npm run build` on your PC.
